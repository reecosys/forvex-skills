# Session output template

The in-chat report, delivered before the carry-forward block. Keep it tight — this is the
summary of a conversation the user just had, not a document.

---

## Week of {CLOSED_WEEK_STAMP} — {DONE} of {SCORED} landed ({RATE}%)

{ONE-LINE VERDICT: e.g. "Strong week — the two that mattered both closed." / "Off-plan: good
work, none of it committed work." / "Third straight week under half; the list is too long."}

**Last week**

| Commitment | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|
| {COMMITMENT} | ✅ done | {optional} |
| {COMMITMENT} | ◐ partial | {what remains} |
| {COMMITMENT} | ✗ missed | {why, one clause} |
| {COMMITMENT} | — dropped | {reason} |

{PATTERN CALLOUT — only when a real pattern exists. One or two sentences, direct.
e.g. "622 Potomac marketing is on its third carry. That's not a scheduling problem — decide
whether it's a priority or drop it."}

**Wins** — {up to three, each with a number}

**Blockers** — {stated as conditions, not feelings}

**Decisions** — {made this week}

---

## Commitments for {NEXT_WEEK_STAMP}

1. {COMMITMENT} — due {DAY}
2. {COMMITMENT} — due {DAY} *(carried {N}×)*
3. {COMMITMENT} — due {DAY}

{TRADE-OFF NOTE if the user cut something to get to 3–5: "You cut {X} to make room. Noted."}

---

## Carry-forward

{One line on where to keep it, matched to what's available: Project knowledge doc, a file, or
paste it into next week's chat.}

{THE FENCED CARRY-FORWARD BLOCK}

Next week I'll open with {THE ONE OR TWO THINGS YOU'LL PRESS ON, named}.

---

## Style reminders

- Verdict first. The user should know how the week went from the first line.
- The table is the review; don't restate it in prose underneath.
- Pattern callout only when there's a real pattern — a forced one reads as nagging.
- No closing pep talk. End on what you'll hold them to.
